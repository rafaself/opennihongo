# NINJAL hentaigana font

Unmodified NINJAL 変体仮名 font, version 1.01, published December 2018 by the
National Institute for Japanese Language and Linguistics (NINJAL).
Its glyphs are a subset of IPAmj Mincho. Used under the Apache License 2.0;
the complete license is retained in LICENSE.txt.

The font contains historical kana glyphs U+1B001–U+1B11E, rather than manuscript
facsimiles. It is loaded locally only when those characters are displayed.
